=== JOURNAL ENTRY RULES ===
JE-101: All software subscription expenses exceeding $1,000 must be debited to Account 5420 (IT Software Operations) and credited to Account 2110 (Accounts Payable).
JE-102: Office supply purchases below $500 should be immediately expensed under Account 5100 (Office Administrative Expenses).
JE-103: Hardware depreciations must use the straight-line method over a 36-month asset lifespan.
